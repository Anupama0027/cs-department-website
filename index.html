About Department

Building Future Technology Leaders

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science is dedicated to excellence in teaching, research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry collaboration. We prepare students to become competent software engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and technology leaders.

Vision

To become a globally recognized centre of excellence in Computer Science education and research.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education.
  • Promote innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Develop ethical professionals.
  • Encourage interdisciplinary research.
  • Strengthen industry interaction.
